About me.

I'm a Data Analyst and Automation Specialist. I turn messy, scattered data into dashboards people trust, and repetitive reporting into systems that run themselves.

My work covers the full path from raw source to decision: Python scrapers and ETL pipelines that collect and clean the data, SQL and MySQL to model it, and Power BI dashboards that decision-makers can read at a glance.

Increasingly, that work includes AI. I use LLMs for sentiment analysis at scale, Whisper to turn audio into searchable text, and MCP servers that connect language models to live sources such as the YouTube API.

I work best with teams that have more data than time. The goal is always the same: fewer manual steps, faster answers, and systems that keep running long after handover.
